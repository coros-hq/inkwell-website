Search looks across every note in your open vault at once.

## Search the whole vault

1. Press <Kbd keys="mod+k" />, or click the **Search** box near the top of the sidebar.
2. Start typing in **Search notes…**.
3. Click a result to open that note. The search window closes.

Press <Kbd keys="esc" /> or click outside the window to close it without picking anything.

Each result shows the note's title, with your search words highlighted, and the path of its file.

## What gets searched

A note matches if your text appears in any of these (capital letters don't matter):

- its **title**
- its **content**
- its **tags**

With nothing typed, the window shows **Recent Notes** so you can jump straight in. When nothing matches, it says **No notes found**.

## Tips

- Search matches the exact text you type, so use a distinctive word rather than a full sentence.
- To search inside a single long note, use <Kbd keys="mod+f" /> instead. See [Find text in a note](/docs/writing/#find-text-in-a-note).
- Search covers the vault you currently have open. To look in another vault, [switch to it first](/docs/vaults-and-files/#switch-vaults).
- Want a different shortcut? Change it in **Settings → Shortcuts**.
