Quick capture is a small pop-up for ideas that can't wait. It works even when inkwell's window is hidden.

## Capture a note

1. Press <Kbd keys="mod+n" /> from any app. A **Quick Note** window opens in the middle of the screen and stays on top of other windows.
2. Type your note. It supports markdown, including the same `/` commands as the main editor.
3. Press <Kbd keys="mod+enter" />, or click **Save**.

The window briefly shows **Saved** and closes. Press <Kbd keys="esc" /> to close it without saving.

  The pop-up's `/` menu leaves out `/chart`, `/board` and `/task`, because there's no full editor behind it.
</Callout>

## Choose where it goes

The name at the top-left of the window is the vault your note will be saved in. By default it's the vault you last used.

- Click the name to pick another vault from your recent vaults.
- Choose **Choose another folder…** to use any folder.

## How your note is saved

Captured notes land in a folder called **Quick Notes** inside the chosen vault. If the folder doesn't exist, inkwell creates it. Each note is an ordinary `.md` file:

- The **title** is the first `# Heading` if you wrote one, otherwise the first line (up to 80 characters), otherwise "Quick Note".
- The **file name** is the title plus a timestamp, for example `buy-milk-1768473000000.md`, so nothing is overwritten.

You don't need the vault to be open in the main window, so you can capture into one vault while working in another.

## Open the pop-up from the menu bar

inkwell puts an icon in your menu bar (macOS) or system tray (Windows and Linux). Click it for:

| Item | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Open inkwell** | Brings the main window forward. |
| **New Quick Note** | Opens the capture pop-up. |
| **Quit inkwell** | Closes the app completely. |

<Callout type="warning" title="Closing the window doesn't quit inkwell">
  When you close the main window, inkwell hides itself and keeps running, so the shortcut still works. To really quit, use **Quit inkwell** in the tray menu, or <Kbd keys="mod+q" /> on macOS.
</Callout>

## If the shortcut doesn't work

<Kbd keys="mod+n" /> is registered system-wide. If another app has already claimed it, inkwell can't register it. Use the tray icon instead, or free up the shortcut in the other app. See [Troubleshooting](/docs/troubleshooting/).
