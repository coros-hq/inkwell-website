Notes rarely stand alone. inkwell lets you link a note to other notes and boards so related work is one click away.

## Open the Links panel

Click the link icon (**Links**) at the top-right of any note. A panel opens on the right. Click it again, or the close button, to hide it.

## Link a note or board

1. In the Links panel, click **Add link…**.
2. Type in **Search notes and boards…**. Results are grouped under **Notes** and **Boards**.
3. Click the one you want. It's added to the panel.

Your links appear in two lists, **Notes** and **Boards**. For each one you can:

- Click its name, or the arrow (**Open note** or **Open board**), to jump to it.
- Click **Remove link** (the ×) to unlink it. This only removes the link; it never deletes the note or board.

If you haven't added anything yet, the panel says **No links yet.**

## Mention a note while you write

You can also link as you type. Type `@` and the start of a note's title, then pick it from the list. inkwell inserts a link into your text and adds that note to the Links panel automatically.

The link is stored as plain markdown, so it survives in the file:

```markdown
See [@Weekly planning](note://note-1234567890) for details.
```

Hold <Kbd keys="mod" /> and click a mention to open that note.

## Link to a board or task from text

From the editor you can also insert a board or task as a live embed or as an inline link. See [Boards and tasks](/docs/boards-and-tasks/#put-a-board-or-task-inside-a-note).

## Where links are stored

The list in the Links panel is kept in the vault's hidden `.inkwell/app.json`, not in the note's frontmatter. Mentions you typed with `@` live in the note text itself.
