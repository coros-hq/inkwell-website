## Customise the shortcuts

Six of the shortcuts can be changed in **Settings → Shortcuts**: **Search**, **Toggle Sidebar**, **Open File**, **Find in Note**, **New Note** and **New Folder**. See [Settings reference](/docs/settings/#shortcuts). The rest are fixed.

<Callout type="note">
  <Kbd keys="mod+n" /> is the system-wide quick capture shortcut. That's why **New Note** inside the app uses <Kbd keys="mod+shift+n" /> instead.
</Callout>
