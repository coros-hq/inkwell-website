A note can have **child notes**, and those can have children of their own, as deep as you like. It works like a GitHub issue with sub-issues: one parent note holds the overview, and each child note holds one piece of the work.

Nesting is separate from folders. A folder groups notes by *where they live*; nesting says *which note a note belongs to*. Use whichever fits, or both: nested notes sit inside whatever folder their parent is in.

## Create a sub-note

- In the note list, hover a note and click **+** (**New sub-note**).
- Or open the note and click **New sub-note** in the top bar of the editor.

The new note opens right away, nested under the note you started from.

## Nest an existing note

In the note list, drag a note and drop it **on top of another note**. It becomes that note's child, along with any notes nested under it. If you've selected several notes, they all move together.

inkwell won't let a note become its own child or sit under one of its own descendants.

<Callout type="note">
  If the note you drop onto is in a different folder, the dropped note moves into that folder too, so a child always lives in the same folder as its parent.
</Callout>

## Move a note back out

- Click **Move out of parent** on the note's row. It appears when you hover the row.
- Or drag the note onto the heading at the top of the note list.

The note goes back to the top level of its folder.

## Expand and collapse

A note with children shows a chevron to the left of its title. Click it to hide or show the children. While collapsed, the note shows how many notes are inside it.

Children are indented under their parent and sorted the same way as the rest of the list (by **Date** or **Name**). inkwell doesn't remember which branches you collapsed.

## Find your way up

When you open a nested note, the breadcrumb at the top of the editor shows its whole path: **Folder › Parent › Child**. Click any parent in the path to jump to it.

## Moving and deleting

- **Moving a parent to another folder** moves all of its children with it.
- **Moving a child to a different folder on its own** detaches it from its parent, because a child stays in its parent's folder.
- **Deleting a parent deletes every note nested under it**, at any depth. The confirmation tells you how many notes will go, for example *Delete "Roadmap" and the 4 notes nested under it?* Read it before you confirm.

## How it's stored

Nesting is saved in the note's own file, as one extra line in its frontmatter:

```yaml
---
id: note-abc123
parent: note-xyz789
---
```

`parent` is the `id` of the parent note. The note files stay flat on disk, so nothing is moved or renamed, and a vault from an older version opens unchanged. Shared vaults carry the nesting to everyone who syncs.

If a note's parent can't be found (for example, you deleted the parent file outside inkwell), the note simply shows at the top level.

## Limits

- Nesting is shown in the **note list**. The folder tree in the sidebar and the tab bar are unchanged.
- Notes you opened with **Open file…** can't be nested, because inkwell doesn't write to those files.
